ABOUT MY PROJECT

Make Your Study Life Easier!

Student Study Planner is a website idea that helps students organize their studies in one place.

Students can plan their daily activities, create timetables, set study goals, and check their progress.

The main aim of this project is to help students manage their time and build good study habits.
